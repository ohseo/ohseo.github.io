<b>Welcome!</b> I am a Ph.D. candidate in <a href='ct.kaist.ac.kr'>Culture Technology</a> at <a href='kaist.ac.kr'>Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST)</a>, supervised by Prof. Woontack Woo and Prof. Sang Ho Yoon. My research focuses on hand interactions for immersive augmented and virtual reality (AR/VR), with an emphasis on the precise selection and manipulation of virtual objects. I am passionate about enabling users to interact seamlessly with virtual objects, even in complex AR/VR environments where diverse content coexists. My work integrates sensing technologies, interaction design, and user studies to develop effective interaction strategies for enhanced precision and usability. Looking ahead, I am particularly interested in integrating AR/VR technologies with interactive entertainment, such as games and theme park attractions, to create immersive and magical experiences. Feel free to reach out to learn more about my research and projects.
