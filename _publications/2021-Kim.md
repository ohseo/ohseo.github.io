                        In this poster, we present a digital twin-based mixed reality system for remote collaboration with the size-scaling of the user and the space. The proposed system supports collaboration between an AR host user and a VR remote user by sharing a 3D digital twin of the AR host user. To enhance the coarse authoring of a shared digital twin environment, we provide a size scaling of the digital twin environment with the world-in-miniature view.
