                        An omni-directional (360°) camera captures the entire viewing sphere surrounding its optical center. Such cameras are growing in use to create highly immersive content and viewing experiences. When such a camera is held by a user, the view includes the user's hand grip, finger, body pose, face, and the surrounding environment, providing a complete understanding of the visual world and context around it.
