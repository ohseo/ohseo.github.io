In this paper, we present a prototype system for sharing a user's hand force in mixed reality (MR) remote collaboration on physical tasks, where hand force is estimated using wearable surface electromyography (sEMG) sensor. In a remote collaboration between a worker and an expert, hand activity plays a crucial role. However, the force exerted by the worker's hand has not been extensively investigated.
