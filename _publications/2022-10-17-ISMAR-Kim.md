We propose an AR store authoring system that enables the users to customize a physical space to promote spatial experience in mobile AR shopping. The current state of AR shopping research attempts to enhance the shopping experience by presenting virtual items onto the user's space. However, under the notion that the stores and products are inseparable in creating a holistic shopping experience, we set to make a shopping experience that encompasses both elements.
