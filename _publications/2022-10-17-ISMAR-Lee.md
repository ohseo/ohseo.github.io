We present CARDS, a comprehensive Augmented Reality (AR) docent system to promote informative and engaging art experiences for exhibition visitors. While utilizing AR to add aesthetic visual effects has been actively explored, facilitating visitor engagement in educational exhibitions through AR has been relatively unexplored.
